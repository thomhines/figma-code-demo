Super Nova Smash! is a fast-paced card game for 2-8 players. The goal is to get rid of your cards—and screw over your friends—before they do it to you.

The app is there to tell you when to play, give you random challenges, and generally make your life a panic-inducing nightmare (in a fun way!)

FAQs
How can I get this amazing game?

The app is available now, but it's kinda useless without the cards and the board. Fortunately, those are scheduled to go into production soon! Join the mailing list to get more details as it happens.

Do I have to be in the same location as my friends to play?

Let’s hope your “friends” didn’t hear you ask that. The answer, however, is YES. Players need to be on the same wifi network for their phones to talk to each other, and everybody plays on the same card piles. Fortunately, the game can provide a good distraction since it seems you don’t really like these people.

What if I want don't have wifi? What if I don't have a smart phone?

Super Nova Smash! does require a smart phone, wifi, and our (free) app in order to play. Don’t worry, though, the experience of playing the game is easily worth hundreds or even thousands of dollars.
